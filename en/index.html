Dibujos: Learn How to Draw

Support Privacy Español

Dibujos is a two-year drawing course for iPhone and iPad, in English and Spanish: 141 lessons and 200 drawings where every stroke draws itself in front of you, from the guides to the shading. The first month is free; the full course unlocks with a single payment.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need?

An HB pencil, an eraser and printer paper. Later a soft pencil (2B or 4B) helps with shading, and a fine liner for the ink month. Each drawing lists its materials.

I have never drawn. Is it for me?

Yes. The course starts with how to hold the pencil and draw a straight line, and each month builds on the last. Fifteen minutes a day is enough.

I'm stuck on a step

Pause the animation and drag the slider to see the stroke slowly. What is about to be drawn shows first in red, dashed. If a step is unclear, write to me and I will look at it.

Where are my sketchbook photos?

Only on your device, inside the app. You can delete one by pressing and holding it in Progress › Your sketchbook.

I changed iPhone and can't see my purchase

Go to Progress › Restore purchase, using the same Apple Account you bought with. The purchase works with Family Sharing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course is inside the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore.
