Privacy policy

Dibujos: Learn How to Draw

Support Privacy Español

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In one sentence: Dibujos collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing to any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.

What is stored and where

The app stores on your device what it needs to work: the lessons you have done, the drawings you have finished and how long they took, your practice minutes, your favourites and your daily goal. That file never leaves your device.

Your sketchbook photos

If you want, when you finish a drawing you can save a photo of yours. You choose it with the system photo picker, so the app has no access to your photo library: it only receives the photo you pick. It is stored, scaled down, inside the app on your device and is not sent anywhere. If you delete the photo or the app, it is gone.

Purchases

The full-course purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no data about you or your payment method.

What there isn't

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No ads or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No third-party components that collect data.

Crash reports

If you have "Share With App Developers" turned on, Apple may send me anonymous technical crash reports. Apple handles them and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Since the app collects no personal data, it collects no data from children either.
